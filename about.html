Who we are

Technology built around the people using it.

Teznet Technologies works with businesses and organizations in Uganda and across East Africa. We bring network infrastructure, web and cloud services, creative media, and technology training together under one team.

We begin by understanding the people, processes, and goals behind a project. Then we help choose and deliver the services that fit, whether that means improving connectivity, building a digital experience, communicating a story, or developing practical skills.
